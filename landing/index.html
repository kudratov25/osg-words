Features

Small, calm, and it just keeps working.

Built for people who are busy — not for people who need another app to manage.

Words in your notifications

08:00, 12:00 and 18:00 — the word, its article, the meaning and an example sentence, right in the notification. Nothing to open.

Home & Lock Screen widget

The current word sits where you look a hundred times a day, and switches at the exact moment its notification arrives.

Your reminders, same schedule

Once, daily, weekdays, weekly or monthly. If one lands near a word, you get a single combined notification — not two pings.

A week ahead, always

Every time you open OSG it plans the next 7 days. Forget about it for a few days — your words and reminders still arrive.

Offline, no account needed

Your progress lives on your phone. Your words download once and then work offline; new and corrected words arrive by themselves when you're online — or pull down to update right away.

Smart review

New words first. Seen words come back after about a week, oldest first — never the same word twice in a day.

FAQ

Questions

What level is it for?
You pick your level (A1–C1) and get words at that level and a little above. About 1,000 German and 1,000 English words: everyday vocabulary — home, food, travel, time, money, health and more — plus words for 8 work areas (retail, IT, medicine, hotels & restaurants, construction, logistics, office, care work). Every word comes with an example sentence and its translation (German words with their article).
Do I need to open the app every day?
No. Each time you open it, OSG schedules the next 7 days of words and reminders. If you haven't opened it for a whole week, it sends one friendly nudge.
Can I change the times?
Words arrive at 08:00, 12:00 and 18:00 for now. Your own reminders can be at any time you like, and one close to a word time is merged into a single notification.
Does it work without internet?
Yes, once your words are on the phone. English→German is built in; other languages download once during setup. When you're online the app picks up new and corrected words by itself — or pull down on the Home screen to update right away.
Android or iPhone?
Both. Android has a Home Screen widget; iPhone has Home Screen (small and wide) and Lock Screen widgets.
